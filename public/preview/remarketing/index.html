Three remarketing ads. Pick one, or two to rotate.

These are the small ads that follow someone around the web after they have visited stevenmclark.com.au: on news sites, YouTube, Gmail and most websites, for about a dollar a day. Each one says why the person is seeing it, names the firm and the town, and offers the free 15-minute call. Nothing about anyone's situation, on purpose: Google does not allow remarketing that refers to family, criminal or debt matters, and we would not want it anyway.

Each option is shown in the two shapes Google needs: wide (1200 by 628) and square (1200 by 1200). The words can be changed in minutes; say what you would say differently.

A. The street

You visited stevenmclark.com.au · Still looking for a lawyer in Gawler? · When you are ready, the first 15 minutes are on us. · Button: Book a free call

B. The desk

You looked us up recently · Looking us up is usually the hard part. · A free 15-minute call with Steven. No pressure to go further. · Button: Ask your question

C. The door

You have been on our website · Gawler's law firm since 1985. · Wills, estates, property, business and notary. Free first call. · Button: Call (08) 8522 6025

Our suggestion

Run A and B together and let Google show whichever gets more clicks. A speaks to people who were comparing firms; B to people working up to a call. C is the quieter brand option if you would rather not lead with a question.
